## How to use this site

- **Skip what you know.** Nobody checks, nobody forces. Walk the order, jump over anything you can already do.
- **Check off as you go.** Every page ends with an "I did this" button. Your progress is saved in this browser and shown as dots above.
- **One page is one sitting.** Short concept, one or two videos, hands-on, check-off. Then stop or continue — your call.

## The learning approach in short

This whole site runs on four habits:

- **Ask why first.** For every concept ask: what is it, why does it exist, when do I use it? Syntax is searchable — understanding is not.
- **Plan before code.** Understand the requirement, draw the plan on paper or in a diagram tool, and only then touch the keyboard. Code is the last step.
- **One change at a time.** Ask AI for one small change, review it, test it, then ask for the next. Big batches hide mistakes.
- **You supervise AI.** AI is the worker, you are the supervisor. Never say "AI did this, I don't know" — you own everything you ship.

## What you will build

You start with HTML/CSS and JavaScript, move through React and Python/Flask, learn the database, the terminal, and Git — and finish by building a complete School CRM capstone alone. The captain only sees the demo.
